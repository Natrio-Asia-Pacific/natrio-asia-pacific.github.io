1. Authorised use

This application is intended for authorised evaluation. Use requires permission from Natrio and the camera account owner. Operators must use only the intended devices and approved application features.

This information website does not provide access to a camera or invite visitors to register for the protected application.

2. Experimental results

Detection, counting, plate transcription and AI review can be incomplete or incorrect. Camera position, lighting, occlusion, network conditions and model limitations can affect results.

The application requires an active connection and browser session. It should not be assumed to provide uninterrupted monitoring or a complete historical record.

3. Human assessment

Operators must check automated observations before acting on them. Results must not be treated as an automated safety decision or as a finding about a person's conduct. The application does not provide automated emergency response.

4. Permissions and privacy

Operators must follow approved purposes, relevant workplace notices and permissions, and applicable restrictions on the camera and connected services. Optional cloud review requires its separate consent.

Read the app privacy disclosure for information about camera access, selected-image processing and data handling. Do not disclose credentials or grant camera access through this information website.

5. Support

Contact cherrera@natrio.com for app support or questions about permitted use.
